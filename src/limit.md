Page Limits
===========


Regular Papers - Minimum Submission of 8 pages
----------------------------------------------



Each page in excess of nine (9) pages will be assessed a mandatory $200.00 fee. This fee cannot be waived. Regular papers cannot exceed a total of 14 pages including the pages with mandatory charges.
Transaction Briefs - Minimum Submission of 4 pages
--------------------------------------------------



Each page in excess of four (4) pages will be assessed a mandatory $200.00 fee. This fee cannot be waived. Transaction Briefs cannot exceed a total of 5 pages including the pages with mandatory charges.
