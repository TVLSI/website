Best Paper Nominations
======================



Nominations for the TVLSI Best Paper Award are due on February 15, 2015. 
If you would like to nominate a manuscript for the TVLSI Best Paper Award, 
please visit <http://ieee-cas.org/about/awards>. 
This is a distinguished award and an excellent way for us to recognize members of the TVLSI community. 




The award is based on general quality, originality, contributions, subject matter and timeliness. Anyone who is an author of a paper published in the “Transactions on Very Large Scale Integration Systems” during the three calendar years preceding the award is eligible for nomination.



Submission
----------



Nominations can be submitted online at <http://ieee-cas.org/2015-ieee-circuits-and-systems-society-best-paper-award-nominations-form>. 
Nomination questions can be sent to [cass-awards@ieee.org](mailto:cass-awards@ieee.org)
